Hi! I'm **Shengnan An (安晟男)**, a researcher on the **LongCat Foundation Model Team at Meituan**, which I joined in July 2025 through the Beidou graduate program.

I received my Ph.D. from **Xi'an Jiaotong University** in June 2025, advised by Prof. Nanning Zheng, through a joint training program with **Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA)**. I was a research intern at MSRA from August 2019 to June 2025, working with Jian-Guang Lou and Zeqi Lin.

My research focuses on understanding and improving the capabilities of large language models, with an emphasis on **mid-training data curation, agents for productivity work, and mathematical and general reasoning**. My earlier research covered long-context understanding, in-context learning, code generation, and compositional generalization.
